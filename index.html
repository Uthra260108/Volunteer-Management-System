The objective of this project is to help orphan students by providing proper guidance and introducing them to upcoming technologies. The system aims to connect students with volunteers who can provide educational and career guidance.

About the Project

Software Engineering Project

Volunteer Management System


The Volunteer Management System is an ongoing Software Engineering project designed to help orphan students by providing guidance and awareness about upcoming technologies.


The proposed system connects students and volunteers through a simple platform where students can receive educational guidance, learning resources and technology awareness.
